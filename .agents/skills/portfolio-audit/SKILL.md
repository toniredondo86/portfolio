# Portfolio Audit

## Goal

Review a UX portfolio page and identify the highest-impact improvements that increase clarity, credibility, and usability.

The portfolio belongs to Toni Redondo, a UX Designer.  
The goal is to convince hiring managers and product teams through clear UX thinking and real project impact.

---

## Evaluate

### Clarity of message

Check if the page clearly communicates:

- what the project is
- what problem was solved
- Toni’s role
- why the work matters

Flag vague or generic statements.

### Visual hierarchy

Evaluate:

- heading structure
- section clarity
- scanning experience
- balance between text and visuals

### Readability

Check:

- paragraph length
- text density
- clarity of headings
- scanning speed

Identify long text blocks or repeated ideas.

### Spacing consistency

Evaluate:

- vertical rhythm
- section spacing
- alignment consistency

### Responsive layout

Check for potential mobile issues:

- forced line breaks
- oversized text blocks
- layouts that may collapse poorly on small screens

### Accessibility basics

Check:

- heading hierarchy
- alt text presence
- link clarity
- readable text sizes
- contrast risks

### Case study clarity

Verify that the page clearly explains:

1. Context
2. Problem
3. Role
4. Process
5. Key decisions
6. Outcome
7. Learnings

Flag missing or weak sections.

---

## Output

Provide:

### 1. What works well

Identify strengths in clarity, structure, or presentation.

### 2. Main problems

List the most important issues affecting:

- clarity
- credibility
- usability

Explain briefly why they matter.

### 3. Recommended fixes

Provide concrete improvements focused on:

- UX narrative
- content clarity
- layout improvements
- credibility signals

### 4. Priority order

Rank fixes by impact:

**Critical**  
Issues harming clarity, credibility, or usability.

**Important**  
Improvements that strengthen structure and narrative.

**Nice to improve**  
Visual polish or refinements.

---

## Rules

Focus on improvements that increase:

- clarity
- trust
- usability
- professional credibility

Avoid focusing only on small visual details if bigger narrative or content issues exist.
