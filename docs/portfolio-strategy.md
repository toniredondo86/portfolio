# Portfolio Strategy

## Positioning

Toni Redondo is a UX Designer focused on designing clear, usable digital products, with experience in complex environments and internal tools.

The portfolio should demonstrate:

- structured thinking
- clear UX reasoning
- strong attention to detail
- the ability to simplify complex problems
- real project impact

This portfolio should position Toni as a designer who can work on real products, not just visual interfaces.

---

## Audience

The primary audience is:

- hiring managers
- product design leaders
- product teams
- international clients

They should quickly understand:

- what Toni does
- how he approaches UX problems
- the type of projects he has worked on
- the impact of his work

The portfolio should be easy to scan and easy to understand in the first minute.

---

## Tone

The site should feel:

- calm
- precise
- professional
- trustworthy
- confident but not self-promotional

Avoid:

- exaggerated marketing language
- trendy UI tricks
- unnecessary animations
- decorative design that distracts from content

The interface should feel intentional and restrained.

---

## Content philosophy

The portfolio should feel like **evidence of how Toni works**, not a gallery of screens.

Case studies must focus on:

- the problem
- the reasoning behind decisions
- the constraints of the project
- the impact of the work

Avoid presenting work as a simple collection of UI screens.

---

## What the portfolio should communicate

After exploring the portfolio, a hiring manager should clearly understand:

- Toni can work on complex digital products
- Toni makes thoughtful UX decisions
- Toni understands real product constraints
- Toni contributes to meaningful product outcomes
